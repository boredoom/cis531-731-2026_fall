### ⚠️ Grading & Late Work Policies
- **Constraint Notice:** This schedule guarantees a minimum 9-calendar-day lead time from release to due date for all assignments. It also enforces a strict minimum 6-day gap between assignment due dates to protect student workload. 
  * **Exception 1:** MP 5 is due on Mon 19 Oct to explicitly observe Wildcat Pause Day. 
  * **Exception 2:** There is a 5-day gap between PS 6 and MP 7; this is an explicit exemption to maintain the cadence without forcing an assignment due date on Halloween (Oct 31).
  * **Exception 3 (Fall Break & Self-Service Pacing):** To ensure no assignments are due during Fall Break (Nov 22-29) and to allow maximum self-pacing, all remaining assignments (PS 8 through HW 12) have been batch-released on Wed 28 Oct 2026. The due dates have been shifted to maintain a strict minimum 7-day cadence leading up to Dead Week.
- **Drop Policy:** The highest **7 of your 9 regular assignments** (non-project HWs and MPs) will count toward your final grade. The lowest 2 scores are dropped automatically.
- **Mandatory Milestones:** The Term Project Milestones (PS 4, PS 8, HW 12) are explicitly marked below. These are mandatory and **cannot be dropped**.
- **Zero Late Work Allowed:** Late submissions are not accepted. If an assignment closes, you will receive zero points unless you have a pre-approved, documented OSSA emergency.
